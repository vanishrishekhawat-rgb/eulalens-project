EULALens

EULALens is a multimodal system for making digital agreements easier to understand. Current EULAs contain important information regarding user rights and responsibilities, which are lost between paragraphs of text that only 8% of users actually read. EULALens helps bridge this gap. Each EULA PDF/Text is broken down into categories, which are explained in the form of cue cards, comic strips and short videos.

Abstract

Digital agreements govern online access. However, due to their length and legal complexity, they are often overlooked, leaving users blind to terms with practical consequences. Ofcom reports that only 8% of online platform users read platform terms in full, while Pew Research found that 56% of U.S. adults frequently accept privacy policies without reading them. We introduce EULALens, an interactive platform that converts EULAs into multimodal explanations: cards, comics, and videos. A user study with 30 participants found the generated formats understandable, accurate, and strongly preferred over the original text, suggesting multimodal AI explanations can make agreements more accessible
