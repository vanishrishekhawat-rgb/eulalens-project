EULALens

Visualizing complexity and extracting actionable insights from legal documents.

Abstract

Digital agreements govern online access. However, due to their length and legal complexity, they are often overlooked, leaving users blind to terms with practical consequences. Ofcom reports that only 8% of online platform users read platform terms in full, while Pew Research found that 56% of U.S. adults frequently accept privacy policies without reading them. We introduce EULALens, an interactive platform that converts EULAs into multimodal explanations: cards, comics, and videos. A user study with 30 participants found the generated formats understandable, accurate, and strongly preferred over the original text, suggesting multi-modal AI explanations can make agreements more accessible.
